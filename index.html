French press, done right

Four minutes is all it takes. Follow the steps and start the timer when you pour.

  1. Grind 30 g of beans coarse, like sea salt.
  2. Add 500 ml of water just off the boil.
  3. Stir once, set the lid on and start the timer.
  4. Press down slowly and pour right away.
